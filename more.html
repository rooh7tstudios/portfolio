know me

age 30.000000000
i didn't plan on becoming a network security engineer.

it started with a simple question: how does all of this actually connect? networks. systems. people. the links nobody notices until they break. that curiosity pulled me in. 5+ years later, i'm the one mapping the flow, spotting the weak links, and making sure things run the way they're supposed to, not the way attackers hope they will.

along the way, i earned a master's in management information systems from the university of illinois chicago, usa, and before that, a bachelor of technology in computer science from gitam university, visakhapatnam. i'm also ccna certified (cisco certified network associate): part engineer, part keeper of the blinking lights. my job is keeping things up and running.

faith in god and curiosity drive me. faith keeps me grounded. curiosity keeps pulling me forward.

so i build, i learn, and i question things. if i've done something twice, i script it.

between clean network diagrams and late-night troubleshooting, i share what actually works. i skip theory that only sounds smart.

pursuits

what started as curiosity about how packets move across networks became a full career. 5+ years deploying palo alto ngfw, cisco asa, arista switching, and zero trust architectures. against a 20-year career arc, it's still early days.

if a task happens more than twice, i automate it, and n8n is my primary tool. i've built pipelines for content scheduling, receipt processing, birthday messages, and more. these days i spend most of my thinking time on ai and security engineering.

shooting with a sony alpha a6700. started with street photography and architecture. you can follow the photography work at @travellin.soldier →

training is a non-negotiable part of my week. mostly compound lifts. consistency over intensity.

semifinalist in team doubles at UIC's 2024 intramural tournament. the only sport where i actually enjoy the competitive pressure.

long-term focused. heavily influenced by naval ravikant's philosophy on wealth creation: own equity, not time.
